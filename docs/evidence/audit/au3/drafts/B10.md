## Why
B9 improved DEV recall at 0.5 from 0.6624 to 0.6735 (145 more roofs of 13,156), 350 roofs short of the 0.70 target.
It changed two things together: three extra RAMP regions (Dhaka, Barishal, Jashore) and scale + colour augmentation.
The gain sits in roofs of 512 source pixels and more, and recall **fell in every size bin under 512 pixels**
(`[16,64)` 0.1269 to 0.0725). Hypothesis: the scale augmentation (factor 0.75 to 1.33; amendment 1 drops an instance
left with no mask pixel) shrinks small roofs out of the training signal, while the extra regions carry the gain.
One run with the regions kept and scale off tests that, with everything else equal.
